# Varia Studio

## Company profile

**Varia Studio is an independent applied agricultural technology studio.** We bring together agricultural science, research, data, AI and software to develop practical responses to real challenges across agriculture.

Our work spans the path from understanding a problem to researching an approach, developing and testing technology, and integrating useful solutions into real workflows. We collaborate with companies, research groups and specialist practitioners across borders.

### Our ambition

To become a trusted technology partner in the agricultural ecosystem: developing useful technologies, adapting promising approaches to local agricultural realities, and connecting expertise across research and industry.

### How we create value

Varia works across three connected modes:

1. **Develop** — research, prototype and engineer new digital and data-driven approaches to agricultural problems.
2. **Adapt** — evaluate and tailor existing technologies, methods and workflows to specific crops, environments, data conditions and operational needs.
3. **Connect** — bring together domain knowledge, technical specialists, research institutions and commercial teams to deliver work that no single discipline could complete alone.

These are not separate businesses. They are complementary ways of solving agricultural technology problems.

### Areas of work

- Agricultural data and decision-support systems
- Soil, crop and environmental information workflows
- Applied AI, machine learning and computer vision
- Remote-sensing, geospatial and field-data applications
- Research, technical feasibility and prototype evaluation
- Backend software, APIs and integration of digital tools

The exact technical scope is set per engagement. Advanced geospatial infrastructure, industrial IoT, production-scale ML, and enterprise platforms may require specialist delivery partners.

### Who we collaborate with

- AgTech and agricultural technology companies
- Agribusinesses and agricultural service providers
- Research institutions, laboratories and universities
- Geospatial, remote-sensing and environmental technology firms
- Specialist engineers, scientists and implementation partners

### Engagement models

- **Applied R&D projects:** investigate a defined problem, assess data and methods, and deliver a tested prototype or technical report.
- **Technology implementation:** build a bounded software, data or integration component against agreed acceptance criteria.
- **Technical subcontracting:** contribute a clearly scoped capability within a partner's larger client project.
- **Collaborative pilots:** combine Varia's agricultural domain perspective with a client's data, field access or specialist capabilities to test a solution.

Every engagement should have a written scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, timeline, fee, assumptions and exclusions.

### How we work

1. Understand the agricultural or operational problem.
2. Review available data, constraints and existing methods.
3. Decide whether the work is feasible and what expertise is required.
4. Agree scope, deliverables, timeline and commercial terms.
5. Develop, test and document the agreed work.
6. Hand over results, limitations and recommended next steps.

### What clients can expect

- Direct founder involvement
- Agricultural domain context alongside technical execution
- Clear communication about evidence, uncertainty and limitations
- Defined scopes and documented deliverables
- Transparent use of specialist collaborators when required

### Current studio profile

Varia is founder-led and intentionally lean. The founder holds an MSc in Soil Resources Management and has practical project experience in agricultural data, applied machine learning prototypes, and Python/Django REST API development. The studio does not claim a large in-house engineering team or enterprise-scale production capability.

Varia's public technical work is presented as prototype or research evidence where appropriate; internal benchmarks are not represented as independent field validation or customer outcomes.

### International operations

Varia seeks international, non-Iranian clients and collaborators. The founder is based in Iran. Contracting, service eligibility and payment routes must be checked for each engagement with the relevant professional and payment-provider requirements before a commitment is made. Varia will not misrepresent its location or use arrangements intended to bypass restrictions.
