# Varia Studio — Sales Operating System
**Internal operating document | 14 November 2026**

## 1. Commercial thesis
Sell a scoped engineering outcome, not an experimental AI model. Use Vision and Carbon as demonstrations of technical capability. The paid offer should map to an existing project, operational bottleneck or customer commitment.

## 2. ICP v1
Primary: AgTech, drone-data, GIS and geospatial service companies with active agricultural projects and an identified need for image analytics, spatial data processing, backend/API work or ML integration.

Buyer roles:
- Founder / CEO at smaller technical service firms
- CTO / Head of Engineering
- Head of Geospatial / Remote Sensing
- Head of Product / Data / AI
- Delivery or Innovation Director

Disqualify or defer:
- no agricultural/geospatial business line;
- no identifiable workflow gap;
- no evidence of current project or budget;
- asks for free custom validation or unpaid integration;
- no decision-maker access;
- expects production SLA, field accuracy or registry-grade MRV unsupported by current product evidence.

## 3. Account qualification score
Score each from 0 to 5:
1. Current problem evidence
2. Urgency / active project trigger
3. Budget or procurement evidence
4. Decision-maker access
5. Delivery fit within 2–6 weeks

Priority:
- 18–25: qualified outbound / discovery
- 13–17: nurture and gather evidence
- 0–12: no outbound

Record the evidence behind every score. Do not award points based on assumptions.

## 6. Better offer design
### A. Applied AI / Geospatial Engineering Sprint
A fixed-scope engineering engagement for an existing customer project:
- workflow and data audit;
- data pipeline / geospatial processing;
- computer-vision or ML evaluation/integration;
- API/backend or dashboard implementation;
- technical handover and risk register.

### B. Computer Vision Evaluation
Only for accounts with a specific image-based use case:
- suitability assessment;
- agreed sample size and evaluation protocol;
- annotated outputs / error analysis;
- fit-gap and integration recommendation.

### C. Geospatial / Soil Data Engineering
For soil, environmental and remote-sensing teams:
- data ingestion, quality control and transformations;
- spatial database / API;
- raster/vector workflows;
- environmental data integration;
- dashboard/reporting.

Every proposal must state assumptions, exclusions, acceptance criteria, data rights, timeline, price, payment milestones, change-control and support boundaries.

## 7. Outreach quality gate
Before sending:
- Can we name the recipient’s specific service/product/customer workflow?
- Is the problem likely important enough to have budget?
- Can Varia deliver the promised result today?
- Does the email offer a business outcome rather than ask the prospect to validate our model?
- Is the ask low-friction and relevant to the recipient’s role?
- Are all technical limitations and data requirements accurate?
- Is there a reason to contact now?

If fewer than 5 of 7 are satisfied, do not send.

## 8. Pipeline stages
1. Research
2. Qualified
3. Contacted
4. Engaged
5. Discovery
6. Scoped opportunity
7. Proposal
8. Contracted
9. Delivery
10. Invoice / cash collected
11. Expansion / referral
12. Closed lost / nurture

A message sent is not traction. A positive reply is not a qualified opportunity. A signed SOW is not cash collected.

## 10. Commercial safeguards
- No free custom report or production integration.
- Small free samples only when they are generic, capped, reusable and require no customer-specific engineering; prefer a short call or public demo instead.
- Require deposit / milestone payment before work starts.
- No unlimited revisions.
- No guarantee of agronomic, yield, carbon-credit or field-accuracy outcomes without appropriate evidence.
- Confirm lawful payment, contracting and data-transfer arrangements before signing.
- Do not send follow-ups simply because silence feels uncomfortable. Follow up only when there is a clear next step or new information.
